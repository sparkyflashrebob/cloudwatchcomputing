Launch Live App in Browser ➔

📱 Android & Samsung Galaxy

  1. Open cloudwatchcomputing.com/flood/ in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
  2. Tap the blue "Install" banner at the top, or tap the three dots menu (⋮).
  3. Select "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
  4. Launches fullscreen with instant notification support.

🍎 Apple iPhone & iPad

  1. Open cloudwatchcomputing.com/flood/ in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (box with upward arrow at bottom).
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
  4. Tap "Add" in the top right corner.

💻 Mac & Windows Desktop

  1. Open in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the small Install icon (computer monitor with arrow) in the address bar.
  3. Click "Install" to run as a dedicated desktop utility.
